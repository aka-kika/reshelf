Notarized Developer ID build for macOS 14 or newer. Open the DMG and drag reshelf to Applications (replace the old one).

Fixed in 1.17.1
- **The agent skill speaks about you, not about the app's maker.** Repos with the UI mark now show "UI: you like its UI" to your agent. Install the skill again from Settings → Library → Agent Skill to get it.

New in 1.17.0: My Repos and Project fits
- **Your own repos in the sidebar.** Press the + next to My Repos and tick your GitHub repos. Without a GitHub token you see your public repos by typing your user name; with one, your private repos too.
- **See what on your shelf fits.** Click one of your repos: the list shows the repos that really fit it, best first, from Top Shelf and The Collector. The inspector shows Why it fits on top, one line written on your Mac by Apple Intelligence.
- **Only when you ask.** Fits are found once when you add a repo. After that, New Fits looks only at repos you saved since, and Analyze All Fits looks at your whole shelf (right-click the repo, or the Shelf menu). Nothing runs in the background, and Stop ends a run.
- **Your rules.** Settings → Fits: how many fits (10 to 25), same language only, recently updated, hide GPL and AGPL, hide archived.
- **It remembers.** Each repo keeps its last list and the time it ran. A dot marks fits that are new since the run before.
- **Hide what does not fit.** Right-click a fit and choose Hide from its list. Show Hidden Fits brings it back.
- **Safe to remove.** Remove from Sidebar takes your repo and its list away after a backup. Your shelf is never touched, and backups and exports carry your repos and their fits.
- **Reshelfed.** When an agent borrows code from a shelf repo for one of your projects, it thanks the author in that project's ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS.md and reshelf shows "Reshelfed in <project>". The Reshelfed row in the sidebar counts them.
- **The UI mark and a Language filter.** Mark the repos whose UI you like (right-click, the brush), and filter any list by language or UI only, next to Sort.
- **A tidier right-click menu.** The shelves and the UI mark as icons on top, then Folder, your clone, and Remove last.
