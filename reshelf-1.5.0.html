Added

  • reshelf updates itself. New versions arrive in the app instead of as a DMG
    • you download and drag. reshelf checks once a day in the background, shows you what changed, and installs only when you say so — nothing happens behind your back. There's a Check for Updates… item in the reshelf menu for when you're impatient, and a switch in Settings ▸ About to turn the automatic checking off. Update checks send nothing about your machine. One catch, once: 1.4.0 has no updater in it, so 1.5.0 is the last version you install by hand.
